# Typical adult reference ranges

These are suggestions shown in the Add Lab Result form. **Every saved result uses the range the user typed from their own report.** Ranges vary by laboratory, method, age, sex and pregnancy status. Values are in `src/lib/catalog.ts`.

## Guideline categories (shown on the test page)

| Test | Categories | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Hemoglobin A1C | Normal < 5.7 % · Prediabetes 5.7–6.4 % · Diabetes ≥ 6.5 % | ADA Standards of Care 2026 |
| Fasting glucose | Low < 70 · Normal 70–99 · Prediabetes 100–125 · Diabetes ≥ 126 mg/dL | ADA Standards of Care 2026 |
| Total cholesterol | Desirable < 200 · Borderline 200–239 · High ≥ 240 mg/dL | NCEP ATP III |
| LDL | Optimal < 100 · Near optimal 100–129 · Borderline 130–159 · High 160–189 · Very high ≥ 190 mg/dL | NCEP ATP III |
| HDL | Low < 40 (men) / < 50 (women) · Protective ≥ 60 mg/dL | NCEP ATP III / AHA |
| Triglycerides | Normal < 150 · Borderline 150–199 · High 200–499 · Very high ≥ 500 mg/dL | NCEP ATP III |
| eGFR | G1 ≥ 90 · G2 60–89 · G3a 45–59 · G3b 30–44 · G4 15–29 · G5 < 15 | KDIGO 2024 CKD guideline |
| Vitamin D 25-OH | Deficient < 20 · Insufficient 20–29 · Sufficient 30–100 ng/mL | Endocrine Society |
| Vitamin B12 | Deficient < 200 · Borderline 200–300 pg/mL | NIH Office of Dietary Supplements |
| hs-CRP | Lower risk < 1 · Average 1–3 · Higher > 3 mg/L | AHA / CDC |

## Suggested ranges by category

Sources: MedlinePlus Medical Encyclopedia, Mayo Clinic Laboratories and common Labcorp/Quest reference intervals. M/F = male/female.

- **Blood sugar:** glucose fasting 70–99 mg/dL · random 70–139 · A1C < 5.7 % · eAG < 117 mg/dL · insulin (fasting) 2.6–24.9 µIU/mL · C-peptide 0.8–3.85 ng/mL
- **Lipids (mg/dL):** total < 200 · LDL < 100 · HDL ≥ 40 M / ≥ 50 F · TG < 150 · non-HDL < 130 · VLDL 5–40 · ApoA1 101–178 M / 116–209 F · ApoB < 90 · Lp(a) < 75 nmol/L
- **CBC:** WBC 4.0–11.0 ×10³/µL · RBC 4.14–5.80 M / 3.77–5.28 F ×10⁶/µL · Hgb 13.2–16.6 M / 11.6–15.0 F g/dL · Hct 38.3–48.6 M / 35.5–44.9 F % · MCV 80–100 fL · MCH 27–33 pg · MCHC 32–36 g/dL · RDW 11.6–15.4 % · Plt 150–450 ×10³/µL · MPV 7.5–12.5 fL
- **Differential:** neutrophils 40–70 % · lymphocytes 20–40 % · monocytes 2–10 % · eosinophils 1–6 % · basophils 0–2 % · ANC 1.5–7.8 · ALC 0.85–3.9 · AMC 0.2–0.95 · AEC 0.015–0.5 · ABC 0–0.2 (×10³/µL)
- **Kidney:** creatinine 0.74–1.35 M / 0.59–1.04 F mg/dL · BUN 6–24 mg/dL · eGFR ≥ 60 · BUN/Cr 10–20 · uric acid 3.7–8.6 M / 2.7–7.3 F mg/dL
- **Electrolytes:** Na 135–145 · K 3.5–5.2 · Cl 98–107 · CO₂ 22–29 mmol/L · Ca 8.6–10.3 mg/dL
- **Liver:** ALT 4–36 U/L · AST 8–33 U/L (MedlinePlus; many labs use upper limits of 40–55) · ALP 44–147 U/L · total bilirubin 0.1–1.2 · direct 0.0–0.3 · indirect 0.1–1.0 mg/dL · albumin 3.5–5.0 · total protein 6.0–8.3 · globulin 2.0–3.5 g/dL · A/G 1.1–2.5
- **Thyroid:** TSH 0.45–4.5 mIU/L · free T4 0.8–1.8 ng/dL · total T4 4.5–12.0 µg/dL · free T3 2.0–4.4 pg/mL · total T3 80–200 ng/dL · TPO Ab < 9 IU/mL · Tg Ab < 4 IU/mL (antibody cutoffs vary widely by assay)
- **Iron:** ferritin 24–336 M / 11–307 F ng/mL · iron 60–170 µg/dL · TIBC 240–450 µg/dL · transferrin 200–360 mg/dL · saturation 20–50 %
- **Vitamins:** B12 200–900 pg/mL · folate ≥ 3 ng/mL · vitamin D 30–100 ng/mL · A 20–60 µg/dL · B1 70–180 nmol/L · B6 5–50 µg/L · C 0.4–2.0 mg/dL · E 5.5–17 mg/L
- **Inflammation:** CRP < 10 mg/L · hs-CRP < 3.0 mg/L · ESR 0–15 M / 0–20 F mm/hr
- **Other:** Mg 1.7–2.2 · phosphorus 2.5–4.5 mg/dL · amylase 40–140 · lipase 13–60 · LDH 105–333 · CK 39–308 M / 26–192 F U/L
